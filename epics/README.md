# Epics

Every epic across the [Al-Ummah-Now](https://github.com/Al-Ummah-Now) repositories, whatever its state: shipped, in progress, planned, deferred or rejected. Owner decisions are in the [decision log](decisions.md).

Collected on 2026-11-10 from:

- each repo's default branch (AlUmmahNow at [`5681162`](https://github.com/Al-Ummah-Now/AlUmmahNow/tree/56811623fceb866a556aabaef1bb6bc8bf6a1362); other repos' SHAs are on their epic pages),
- every issue and PR in the org that mentions an epic or story (a full GraphQL listing of 401 issues and 817 PRs, not the capped search API),
- the project's chat threads, for epics and decisions that only exist there.

Links to repo files point at a fixed commit, so they show what the page was written from. The source wins when this page and the source disagree.

**Status** uses five values. *Shipped* means the code is on `main` and works on QA; any sign-off gaps are noted on the page. *In progress* means some stories have shipped or have open PRs. *Planned* means specified with no code yet. *Deferred* means an owner decision or the roadmap postponed it. *Rejected* means it was decided against.

**IDs.** `E01`–`E30` are the monorepo's numbered epics (Epics 1–30). No number above 30 is used for an Al Ummah Now epic in any repo doc, issue, PR or chat thread. The `wt-epics58` checkout means "Epics 5–8", and the "Epic 59.03" and "Epic 40" mentions in chat belong to other projects. Other IDs are prefixed by origin: `LR-` and `AR-` for legacy roadmap items, `AUN-` for monorepo workstreams with a spec but no epic number, and a repo prefix for epics defined in another repo. Epics that exist only in chat are marked *(chat only)*.

## Conflicts found while collecting

Each epic page lists its own conflicts. The cross-cutting ones:

- **Decision IDs collide.** `docs/embeds/PLAN.md` numbers its decisions D1–D12, the same IDs as the native-apps decisions D1–D11. This wiki prefixes the embeds ones as `EMB-D*`.
- **Team roles story number.** Epic 29 and the Epics 5–8 audit call team roles "native 19.6". `EPICS.md` numbers them 19.8, and 19.6 is the analytics deep dive.
- **Duplicate "Epic 7" headings.** `docs/archive/epics-original.md` has three "Epic 7" headings, and two of them hold the Epic 6 and Epic 8 stories.
- **Look-alike IDs.** `[Phase N.M]` issues (#45–#71) and `[Phase 2] Task 2.N` issues (#126–#140) look like epic.story IDs but are not.
- **Cost cap.** The social media spec estimates ≈ $620–720/month with X, and the Tools roadmap budgets $5,000–10,000/month for infrastructure. Both are over the ≤ $500/month whole-platform cap.
- **Tier models.** Epic 27 seeds Free / Plus / Pro / Institution plans, where Free can upload. The owner's 2026-11-10 chat rule makes uploads invite-only, with admin-assigned creator tiers None / Creator / Unlimited, and that is what is built.
- **Language defaults.** Sign-in and profile pages are English unless `?lang=ar` (O21). The account hub spec keeps a `lang` cookie and adds a stored-locale default, even though the hub *is* the profile app. AlUmmahNowDesktop says "Arabic default". www and the CMS admin also use a cookie and Accept-Language, but those are different surfaces.
- **Stale plan statuses in other repos.** The Auth roadmap says 0/5 for every epic, both OpenAuth fork plans say "PLANNING", the Tools consolidation plan says "Proposed", and the CMS roadmap lists shipped work as queued. Each page takes its status from the newer evidence and names the stale doc.
- **Epic 18 vs web Studio.** In chat the owner took Epic 18 to be the web Studio. Epic 18 is Studio mobile, and the web Creator Studio has no epic number.
- **Two crates claim story 11.4.** In AlUmmahNowCore, `aun-upload` and `aun-i18n` both say story 11.4.
