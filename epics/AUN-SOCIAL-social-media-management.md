## Goal

Channel teams connect Facebook, X, Telegram, Instagram, YouTube and others in Studio to auto-announce, cross-post, schedule, measure and answer.

## Scope

- Auto-post to **X, Telegram, Bluesky and Mastodon**. No Facebook, Instagram or Threads ([SM-6](decisions.md#sm-6-register-a-business-for-meta): *"no auto post to meta"*)
- Scheduler tools (*"if someone uses hootsuite or similar we need to be able help them somehow"*): RSS feeds per channel, Open Graph/Twitter/video tags and oEmbed, signed webhooks, a Studio share kit, and a help page for Hootsuite and Buffer
- Default post format is the video itself, natively; link posts opt-in (owner decision 2)
- Always async: queues, retry, DLQ, admin errors page (owner decision 5)
- Lives in Creator Studio as a **Social** rail item; follows per-channel roles (#462)

## Decisions and constraints

- [D2](decisions.md#d2-video-delivery): Video delivery (decided), see the [D2 page](D2-video-delivery.md)

Platform-wide constraints that apply to every epic:

- Whole-platform infrastructure cost cap of **≤ $500/month** ([O1](decisions.md#o1-whole-platform-cost-cap), [D2](D2-video-delivery.md)).
- Payments go through pluggable providers; **never Stripe** ([O4](decisions.md#o4-pluggable-payment-providers-never-stripe-admin-managed-plans), [T2](decisions.md#t2-billing-provider)).
- Every feature is tiered, with plans **custom and admin-managed** ([T1](decisions.md#t1-plans-are-admin-managed)). Uploads and channel creation are invite-only through admin-assigned creator tiers until subscriptions exist ([O25](decisions.md#o25-invite-only-uploads-with-admin-assigned-creator-tiers)).
- UI is bilingual en/ar with RTL. Sign-in and profile pages default to **English unless `?lang=ar`** ([O21](decisions.md#o21-english-default-on-sign-in-and-profile)).

## SOC-1: what is built

- **Connect in Studio → Social**, per channel: X, Bluesky and Mastodon through OAuth (PKCE; Bluesky with DPoP). The link is made only when the signed-in person who started it confirms on return. Telegram works through a one-time code posted by a chat admin. Tokens are sealed with AES-256-GCM (`social-token-kek[-qa|-stage]`, generated by the Provision generated secrets workflow).
- **Rules per account and event** (video, Short, live, community post), posted as the video itself or as a link. Post text templates in English and Arabic, cut to each network's limit.
- **Async delivery**: the processor queue with retry/backoff and a DLQ. Studio's Recent posts shows each failure's reason and **Try again**. Failures are flagged on the admin Errors page with Retry.
- **Admin → Social posting**: kill switch and per-network switches, the X monthly spend cap (default $0, max $500; posts with a link pause from 90% of the cap), the X developer app and Telegram bot setup, 7-day activity and recent failures. Changes are audited.
- **Scheduler tools**: RSS 2.0 feeds per channel (everything, videos, Shorts, posts, live; `?lang=ar`) with a thumbnail enclosure and **Copy feed URL**. Link previews and oEmbed for videos and channels. Signed webhooks (HMAC, retries, a test ping, secret rotation).
- **Share kit** on each video in Studio: post text and link in English and Arabic, a thumbnail download, and for long videos an optional vertical clip from one of the channel's Shorts.
- **Help**: the *Social accounts and scheduler tools* topic, covering Hootsuite, Buffer, Zapier, Make and IFTTT.
- A deleted channel takes all its social rows with it.
- **Cost**: X charges $0.015 per post, $0.20 with a link, up to the admin cap (default $0). Telegram, Bluesky and Mastodon are free. Queue and D1 use is negligible.
- **Setup the owner does once**: an X developer app with the callback `https://videos.api.alummahnow.net/api/social/oauth/x/callback` (and the qa/stage hosts), its client ID and secret entered in admin; a Telegram bot from BotFather, its token entered in admin.
