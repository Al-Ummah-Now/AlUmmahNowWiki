## 1. Goal

Provide a safe, calm, bilingual (English/Arabic) **Ummah Kids** viewing experience owned entirely by the parent's account, collecting **no personal data** from children:

- **Parent owns everything:** The adult's existing `AlUmmahNowAuth` account holds all settings, parental controls, and the Kids Mode toggle. There are no child sign-in accounts, no child emails, no child birthdates, and no child social graphs.
- **Curated, allowlisted catalog & playlists only:** Children can watch only videos (and curated playlists) explicitly approved by human moderators, filtered by age band (`3–6` and `7–12`, plus catalog tag `all` for items spanning both bands; the **admin catalog** Age band filter defaults to **All age bands** / `"All ages"` view, while parent/session selection uses `'3–6'` or `'7–12'` pending owner confirmation before `0083` ships on whether viewer `active_age_band` stays `'7-12'` or allows `'all'`) and topic.
- **Zero social or outbound surfaces:** In Kids Mode there are **no** comments, **no** uploads, **no** community posts, **no** direct messaging, **no** live chat, **no** live streams, **no** notifications inbox/push banners, **no** likes/dislikes counters, **no** offline downloads and **no** Listen (audio-only) mode ([E14](E14-offline-listen.md) `listenMode` & `offlineDownloads` disabled), and **no** external links or clickable in-video overlays ([AUN-PLAYER-LINKS](AUN-PLAYER-LINKS-in-video-links.md) disabled; URLs stripped from descriptions).
- **Zero tracking & zero child attribution:** Al Ummah Now has no behavioral advertising. In Kids Mode, we enforce a complete write-path and identifier suppression policy across every Worker, web handler, log stream, and native SDK (see [§4.3](#43-complete-write-path--identifier-suppression-inventory)).
- **Parental controls:** Scoped Kids-session lock (see [§4.2](#42-enforcement-model--threat-model-kids-d13)), PIN-protected exit with escalating lockout, daily viewing time limits, bedtime quiet hours, optional prayer-time pauses, content age-band selection, and a calm session-complete screen (autoplay off by default).
- **Curated Kids playlists:** Ordinary platform playlists (built on **staff-owned editorial channels**—an admin-managed list of editorial channel IDs stored in `kids_policy_settings.staff_channel_ids_json` and maintained by super admins) explicitly allowlisted for Kids in `kids_catalog_items`, covering du'a, stories of the prophets, Arabic letters, Qur'an, and character/manners. Within an allowlisted playlist, Kids Mode serves **only individually approved and visible videos** (following the [owner decision to drop separate learning paths](decisions.md#e20-playlists-curated-kids-playlists-replace-learning-paths); see also [Epic 21](decisions.md#e21-no-instruments-drop-instrument-free-and-madhab-filters)).

## 3. Legal, regulatory & child-safety requirements

> Every legal question below maps to a numbered item in [§11 Owner Decisions](#11-owner-decisions) and [§3.7 Questions for the counsel letter](#37-questions-for-the-counsel-letter). No child-data code may ship or `ummahKids` turn ON until legal counsel signs off.

### 3.1 COPPA (United States — 16 CFR Part 312 & 2025 Rule Amendments)

Reference: [16 CFR Part 312 (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312) and FTC COPPA FAQs (`https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions`), including the FTC's 2025 COPPA Rule amendments (effective June 23, 2025; full compliance deadline April 22, 2026).

- **When COPPA applies:** Operators of websites or online services directed to children under 13 (or general-audience services with actual knowledge they collect personal information from a child under 13).
- **Persistent identifiers & operational logs:** Cookies, device IDs, IP addresses, or user IDs tied to viewing behavior count as personal information unless strictly limited to the **support for internal operations** exception (security, fraud/rate-limit protection, network delivery, maintaining site functionality — zero behavioral targeting, zero user-level profiling, zero cross-session tracking).
- **2025 COPPA Rule deliverables (part of `E20-10` even under internal operations):**
  1. **Written children's data retention policy**, published in our online Kids privacy notice, specifying business need and deletion schedule (`KIDS-D1`, `KIDS-D6`).
  2. **Written information security program** covering confidentiality, security, and integrity of any children's or parent-proxy operational data, with annual review.
  3. **Internal-operations transparency notice** identifying the specific internal operations for which persistent identifiers (such as transient HTTP IP addresses or security rate-limit keys) are collected, and confirming they are never used for profiling or contact.
  4. **Separate opt-in consent** before any non-integral disclosure to third parties (none planned; third-party ad/tracker SDKs are excluded).
- **Platform log & retention rule for Kids Mode:** Structured application loggers receiving a Kids Mode session clear both `userId` and dynamic resource IDs (`loggingMiddleware` logs matched route templates such as `/api/videos/:id/view`, and `VideoView` omits `videoId`); Cloudflare Workers platform/CDN IP logs stay governed by short-retention internal-operations configuration (≤ 24 hours where configurable, never joined to viewing analytics).

### 3.2 UK Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code, ICO)

Reference: [ICO Age Appropriate Design Code](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/age-appropriate-design-a-code-of-practice-for-online-services/).

- **Scope is under 18, not under 13:** Applies to services likely to be accessed by children under 18 in the UK.
- **Standards designed into E20:**
  - **Standard 1 & 2 (Best interests & DPIA):** Complete a UK/EU DPIA before launch (`E20-10`).
  - **Standard 4 (Transparency):** Plain-language English & Arabic Kids privacy notice (`E20-10`).
  - **Standard 7 & 8 (High privacy by default & Data minimization):** Profiling, behavioral tracking, notifications, and personalized feeds are off by default.
  - **Standard 10 (Geolocation):** Off by default. Optional prayer-time pauses (`KIDS-D10`) use parent-selected city/timezone settings rather than GPS.
  - **Standard 11 (Parental controls transparency):** Show a visible, child-readable indicator in Kids Mode whenever parental time limits or bedtime controls are active (e.g. "Parental controls are on · 30 min limit" / «الرقابة الأبوية مفعّلة، الحد اليومي ٣٠ دقيقة»).
  - **Standard 12 & 13 (Profiling & Nudge techniques):** Profiling is off. Autoplay-next is **OFF by default**, zero streak pressure or manipulative countdowns, and session end shows a calm break screen.

### 3.3 GDPR-K (EU GDPR Art. 8 & UK GDPR) & Regional / US State Laws

- **EU/UK GDPR:** Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (`https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj`), Articles 8, 12, 17, 20, and 35. Digital consent ages vary (13–16). Even under a parent-owned session, if a child watches under the parent's session and anything is stored per request, counsel must confirm whether that record constitutes the child's personal data (`Q3` below).
- **Complete GDPR export & erasure (`S10`):**
  - `collectUserData()` (`DELETE`/`GET /api/privacy/data`): includes the parent's `parental_controls` settings metadata (age band, daily limit, bedtime config, `prayer_pause_enabled`, `hasPin`, `pinUpdatedAt` — **never** `pin_hash` or `pin_salt`).
  - `eraseUserData()`: deletes `parental_controls` (`WHERE user_id = ?`), tombstones `kids_review_requests.requested_by` & `decided_by` (`deleted-user`), and tombstones `kids_catalog_audit.actor_id` (`deleted-user`) (see [Data Lifecycle guide](../guides/data-lifecycle.md)).
- **MENA / Arabic-majority jurisdictions (`S4`):** Most of our audience uses Arabic. Counsel must assess:
  - **Saudi PDPL** (Personal Data Protection Law & SDAIA rules on minors/guardians);
  - **UAE PDPL** (Federal Decree-Law No. 45/2021);
  - **Egypt Personal Data Protection Law No. 151/2020** (rules on processing children's data).
- **US State privacy & app-store age laws (`S4`):** CCPA/CPRA under-16 opt-in rules, plus state app-store age-verification/accountability laws (Texas, Utah, California, Louisiana) and the matching OS interfaces (Apple **Declared Age Range API** & Google Play **Age Signals API**).

### 3.4 App Store & Google Play Rules (`KIDS-D3`)

- **Apple App Store Review Guidelines:** [App Store Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) §1.3 (Kids Category) and §5.1.4 (Kids):
  - **Metadata rule under Option A:** Apps outside the Kids Category **may not** include terms such as "For Kids" or "For Children" in their app name, subtitle, or primary Kids Category metadata (§1.3). If we ship Option A (Kids Mode inside the general viewer app), our App Store listing description and marketing must frame Parental Controls / Family Mode accordingly rather than claiming Kids Category placement.
  - **Parental gate:** Apple requires a parental gate for external links, purchases, or escaping Kids Category apps. Under Option A inside a general viewer app, our in-app parental gate (PIN + adult challenge) is **our own safety control**, implemented directly in SwiftUI/Compose/Web (Apple provides no system "App Store parental gate" SDK component).
- **Google Play Families Policy:** [Google Play Families Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335):
  - Declaring under-13 ages in Target Audience puts the **entire app binary** under the Families Policy—not just when Kids Mode is active! Mixed-audience apps (13+ and under-13) require a neutral age screen at launch, strict Families-certified SDK rules across the whole app, and restrictions on Advertising ID and persistent device identifiers for **all** under-13 and unknown-age users.
  - General-audience social features (comments, community posts, live chat) can conflict with Families Policy unless gated/separated or the app declares `13+` (`Not designed for children`) and relies on adult-owned parental controls. See **Question 6** for counsel & Store Operations (`KIDS-D3`).

### 3.5 Content safety, `madeForKids` outside Kids Mode (`KIDS-D14`), & Channel/Playlist Semantics (`KIDS-D16`)

- **Moderator-curated allowlist only (`D5`):** A video enters Kids playback only when explicitly approved (`target_type = 'video'`). Ordinary playlists created on **staff-owned editorial channels** (channels listed in `kids_policy_settings.staff_channel_ids_json`) may be allowlisted (`target_type = 'playlist'`) as curated Kids playlists: non-staff/creator playlists are refused on both `POST /api/kids/catalog` and review approval, Studio `POST /api/studio/channels/:channelId/kids-review` accepts `video` only, and **`GET /api/kids/playlists/:id` returns only the playlist's videos that are individually approved for Kids (`target_type = 'video'`, `status = 'approved'`) and visible (`search_videos.visible = 1`)** plus approved-only playlist metadata (cover and count derived solely from approved visible videos; links stripped), silently omitting unapproved items—playlist approval never admits unapproved videos or metadata by itself. Until channel admission semantics are built and shipped, approving a channel admits **no** videos by itself (fail closed): the server rejects `target_type = 'channel'` approvals, `GET /api/kids/catalog` omits `target_type = 'channel'` rows, Videos Admin (`E20-3`) hides channel approvals, and existing or newly uploaded videos from a channel remain excluded until explicitly approved by a human moderator.
- **Automated signals never auto-publish:** `islamic_kids` categories and uploader `madeForKids` flags never auto-admit content to Kids Mode.
- **`madeForKids` outside Kids Mode (`S1`, `KIDS-D14`):** Following the FTC/NY AG 2019 YouTube settlement, child-directed videos on a general-audience site still implicate COPPA/AADC even outside `/kids`. `KIDS-D14` decides whether `madeForKids`-flagged (or Kids-catalog) videos on the general site also disable comments, personalized recommendations, and user watch history.
- **Immediate pull-down, re-review on edit & zero links:** Soft-deleting, unpublishing, flagging in moderation (`E28`), or revoking a video/playlist—or removing a playlist's channel from `kids_policy_settings.staff_channel_ids_json`—excludes it from served Kids pages via live D1 verification on every origin response (within the 60 s public edge `Cache-Control` / invalidation window). **Approved-video edit rule (`S-N`):** editing or replacing an already-approved video's `title`, `description`, localized text, `thumbnailUrl`, media, **audio tracks, captions/subtitles, or chapters** across **every** write path (`videoUpdate.ts`, `videoThumbnailUpload.ts`, `videoBulkUpdate.ts` edit + undo, `lib/admin/service.ts` `updateVideo`, `uploadComplete.ts`, `renditions.ts`, `audioTracks.ts` create/complete/retry/update/delete + `endpoints/audio/upload.ts`, `subtitlesUpload.ts`, `subtitlesSetDefault.ts`, `subtitlesDelete.ts`, `captionDrafts.ts` publish, and `chaptersUpdate.ts`) **synchronously (failing closed before mutating the video object if D1 revocation fails)** sets `kids_catalog_items.status = 'revoked'`, audits `'metadata_changed'` (with edit path reason), and clears the browse snapshot cache. **Edits while in trash:** if the video row in `kids_catalog_items` is currently `'deleted'` with `pre_delete_status = 'approved'`, the edit sets `pre_delete_status = 'revoked'` so restoring it from trash returns it as `'revoked'` for moderator re-review (never `'approved'`). External links/overlays (`AUN-PLAYER-LINKS`) and URLs/bare domains in video/playlist descriptions are stripped cleanly with line breaks preserved.

### 4.2 Enforcement model & threat model (`KIDS-D13`)

A client-only cookie or optional `X-Kids-Session` header sent alongside an adult Bearer token can be bypassed trivially by opening another tab (`www`, `community`, `CMS`, scholar sites, or the `AlUmmahNowAuth` account hub), calling `/session/accounts` or `/session/switch` (`apps/web/src/lib/auth/auth-accounts.ts`), or omitting the header—**even cross-origin**, because `api-videos` CORS runs with `credentials: false` (`apps/api-videos/src/index.ts`), so browsers neither store nor attach `HttpOnly` API-host cookies on cross-origin fetches.

**One single authoritative enforcement model is required (`KIDS-D13`):**

- **Recommended Option A (Full Auth + App Session Downgrade to `kids:viewer`):**
  1. **Lock the Auth sign-in session (`AlUmmahNowAuth`):** Calling `POST /api/me/kids-mode/enter` increments/reads `parental_controls.kids_session_epoch`, inserts a row in `kids_sessions` (`sid`, `user_id`, `device_id`, `epoch`, `age_band`, `expires_at`), marks the active `AlUmmahNowAuth` browser/device session as **kids-locked**, mints a short-lived **`15`-minute** `kids:viewer` access token alongside a **`12`-hour** refresh window checked against `kids_sessions` + `kids_session_epoch` (with `sid`, `epoch`, and validated `deviceId`), and drops adult access/refresh tokens from the client. While locked, Auth silent-authorize requests return **only** `kids:viewer` tokens or refuse, and `/session/accounts` / `/session/switch` are disabled.
  2. **Web transport without cross-origin credential leakage (`apps/web`):** `apps/web` stores the Kids session credential in a **host-only `HttpOnly; Secure; SameSite=Lax` cookie scoped strictly to the `apps/web` origin** (never `Domain=.alummahnow.net` or a parent-domain cookie, so QA/stage `videos.qa.*`/`videos.stage.*` Workers cannot receive or overwrite production credentials; sibling web origins enforce Kids Mode independently through the locked Auth session and `kids:viewer` token scope in steps 1 and 3—if a non-secret UI hint is needed, it uses a separate non-credential per-environment marker), drops all adult tokens from browser storage, and **routes every Kids-Mode API request from the browser through `apps/web` server route handlers** (attaching `kids:viewer` credentials and `X-Kids-Mode: 1` server-side rather than calling `api-videos` directly from the browser—this proxy routing is planned explicitly in `E20-5`). Scripts cannot opt out of Kids Mode by omitting a client header.
  3. **Cross-app refusal & resource-server scope enforcement:** Resource servers (`api-videos`, `admin-api`, and **`websocket-server`**) enforce Kids Mode from the **verified Auth access token's `kids:viewer` scope/claim and active `sid`/`kids_session_epoch` check**—never solely from an optional `X-Kids-Session` header, so omitting the header never bypasses Kids restrictions. **`websocket-server` (`apps/websocket-server/src/auth.ts`) must refuse `kids:viewer` tokens on connect**, so a Kids session can never subscribe to `user:<own id>`, private notifications (`KIDS-D17`), or `live:<streamId>` live chat (`E17`) or publish to any channel. **Every** `AlUmmahNowAuth` sibling web/app client (`AlUmmahNowAuth` account hub, `AlUmmahNowCMS`, `AlUmmahNowUI` `www`, **community**, and **scholar sites**, plus any future Auth client) must refuse `kids:viewer` tokens (`403 kids_mode_restricted`) or redirect the user back to `/kids`. Tokens are immediately revocable via `kids_sessions.revoked_at` + `parental_controls.kids_session_epoch` bump on `exit`, PIN change, or sign-out everywhere.
  4. **Exit & interactive step-up:** `POST /api/me/kids-mode/exit` unlocks the session and restores adult credentials only after verifying the parental PIN (atomic escalating lockout) or an **interactive** Auth SSO re-authentication step-up (`prompt=login`, `auth_time <= 300s`—never silent authorize while the Auth session is kids-locked).
  5. **Threat-model boundary (explicit scope):** Kids Mode protects against a child using an unlocked device who **cannot sign in as the parent**. Because a private window starts signed out and on shared family devices OS passkeys may be unlocked with the device passcode, **a child who knows the parent's password, passkey/device passcode, or OS unlock PIN is out of scope**. **Unlock credential recommendation (`KIDS-D13`):** require a fresh passkey prompt with explicit user verification **plus** the parent's password whenever the passkey is a synced platform passkey (or when a password is configured on the account). On mobile/tablet/TV (`E20-7`, `E20-8`), apps pair the session lock with OS **Guided Access / Screen Pinning** guidance.
- **Alternative Option B (Header / cookie restriction marker alongside adult session — rejected for enforcement):** Issuing a restriction marker header/cookie while adult access/refresh tokens stay live is **only** suitable as an internal backend primitive inside `E20-4` prior to `AlUmmahNowAuth` lock wiring and **must not** ship to users under `KIDS-D13`.

### 4.3 Complete write-path & identifier suppression inventory (`B2`)

Every request carrying an active `kids:viewer` scoped session (and client telemetry/error ingest batches flagged as Kids Mode) is governed by the following complete table. **Story ownership & CI completeness rule:**

## 5. Data model and API

All new tables live in `SEARCH_DB` (`apps/api-videos/migrations/search/`). Every query uses primary keys or composite indexes—never unindexed full-table scans.

### 5.1 Database schema (`SEARCH_DB`)

`0083_ummah_kids.sql` (`E20-2` / `#1780`) creates tables 1–3, 4 (base columns below), and 6; **`E20-4` (`#1801`) adds `parental_controls.kids_session_epoch` and table 5 (`kids_sessions`) via a follow-up migration** once `KIDS-D13` is approved.

1. **`kids_catalog_items`** — Curated allowlist of videos and staff-owned curated playlists (`id`, `target_type` `'video'|'playlist'|'channel'`, `target_id`, `age_band` `'3-6'|'7-12'|'all'`, `status` `'approved'|'revoked'|'deleted'`, `pre_delete_status` `'approved'|'revoked'`, `topics_json`, `position`, `approved_by`, `approved_at`, `deleted_at`, `deleted_by`, `delete_reason`, `purge_after`, `updated_at`; unique `(target_type, target_id)`, indexes `(status, target_type, age_band, position, approved_at DESC, id)` and `(status, purge_after)`). Server curation endpoints (`E20-2`) reject `channel` targets and non-staff playlist targets until channel semantics are implemented.
2. **`kids_review_requests`** — Creator requests for Kids review (`target_type = 'video'` only on Studio submit; `id`, `target_type`, `target_id`, `channel_id`, `requested_by`, `suggested_age_band`, `notes`, `status`, `decision_reason`, `decided_by`, `decided_at`, `created_at`; indexes `(status, created_at, id)`, `(channel_id, created_at)`, `(requested_by)`, `(decided_by)` for indexed GDPR erasure).
3. **`kids_catalog_audit`** — Immutable staff action log (`entity_type`, `entity_id`, `action`, `actor_id`, `details_json`, `created_at`; indexes `(entity_type, entity_id, created_at)`, `(actor_id)` for indexed GDPR erasure).
4. **`parental_controls`** — Parent-owned settings & hashed PIN (zero child PII):
   - `user_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY` (parent's Auth subject ID)
   - `pin_hash TEXT`, `pin_salt TEXT`, `pin_updated_at TEXT` (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 at **`100,000` iterations**, the maximum supported by Cloudflare `workerd`, random 16-byte salt — **never returned by any GET response** and reject hashes requesting `> 100,000` iterations cleanly)
   - `pin_failed_attempts INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0`, `pin_locked_until TEXT`, `pin_hard_locked INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0` (claimed **atomically before** PBKDF2 verification so concurrent bursts cannot bypass lockout thresholds)
   - `active_age_band TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT '7-12' CHECK (active_age_band IN ('3-6', '7-12'))` *(only the admin catalog Age band filter defaults to **All age bands** / `"All ages"` view; whether the viewer session default stays `'7-12'` or allows `'all'` is flagged for explicit owner confirmation before `0083` ships)*
   - `daily_limit_minutes INTEGER` (null = unlimited; 15–240)
   - `bedtime_start TEXT`, `bedtime_end TEXT`, `bedtime_timezone TEXT`
   - `prayer_pause_enabled INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0 CHECK (prayer_pause_enabled IN (0, 1))`
   - `kids_session_epoch INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 1` *(`E20-4` follow-up migration)* — monotonically incremented on `exit`, PIN change, or sign-out everywhere to invalidate outstanding `kids:viewer` tokens immediately
   - `updated_at TEXT NOT NULL`
5. **`kids_sessions`** *(`E20-4` follow-up migration)* — Active/revocable Kids Mode sessions (`sid TEXT PRIMARY KEY`, `user_id TEXT NOT NULL`, `device_id TEXT NOT NULL`, `epoch INTEGER NOT NULL`, `age_band TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (age_band IN ('3-6', '7-12'))`, `expires_at TEXT NOT NULL`, `revoked_at TEXT`, `created_at TEXT NOT NULL`; composite index `(user_id, revoked_at)` for indexed lookup, revocation, and GDPR erasure).
6. **`kids_policy_settings`** — Admin-managed policy readiness record (`key = 'default'`, `decisions_json`, `staff_channel_ids_json`, `legal_approved`, `legal_approved_by`, `legal_approved_at`, `updated_at`).

### 5.2 API surface (`admin-api` staff setup vs `api-videos` feature-gated)

**`admin-api` staff setup & curation routes (NOT gated by `ummahKids` switch — registered as explicit staff-setup exemptions so moderators and admins can curate catalog & record decisions while `ummahKids` is OFF, matching `creatorSupport`; requires `authMiddleware` + `adminMiddleware` + `passkeyMiddleware`; soft deletes retain pre-delete status for restore and are permanently purged after 30 days by hourly `admin-api` cleanup; all writes audited):**
- `GET /api/kids/catalog`, `POST /api/kids/catalog` (rejects `channel`, validates target existence, rejects playlists not on `staff_channel_ids`), `PATCH /api/kids/catalog/:id` (rejects `channel`, validates staff ownership on playlist re-approval), `DELETE /api/kids/catalog/:id` (admin+), `POST /api/kids/catalog/:id/restore` (admin+, re-runs `verifyCatalogTarget` before restoring `approved`; restores `channel` targets and playlists whose channel was removed from `staff_channel_ids` as `revoked`)
- `GET /api/kids/staff-playlists` (autocomplete/listing of playlists belonging to `staff_channel_ids`)
- `GET /api/kids/review-requests`, `POST /api/kids/review-requests/:id/decide` (claims pending row atomically; rejects `channel` & non-staff playlists on approval; preserves existing catalog topics if none supplied)
- `GET /api/kids/policy`, `PUT /api/kids/policy` (`super_admin` + step-up passkey required; partial decision updates + `staffChannelIds`)

**`api-videos` viewer, parent & creator routes (ALL gated by `FEATURE_ROUTES` under `ummahKids`, returning `404 feature_off` when OFF):**
- `GET /api/kids/catalog` — Approved allowlist filtered by `ageBand`, `topic`, `targetType` (`video` or `playlist`), and allowlist-scoped search `q` (`q` matches across the active approved catalog without unindexed full-table scans). **Omits `target_type = 'channel'` rows until channel admission semantics are built and shipped**, re-checks that every allowlisted playlist's `channel_id` is still in `kids_policy_settings.staff_channel_ids_json` and `search_playlists.visible = 1`, and omits playlists that currently have zero approved visible videos. Videos strip URLs from `description` & `descriptionLocalized` in addition to omitting `playerLinks`, `endScreen`, and `cards`; playlist cards use the sanitized **Kids playlist shape** below. When called inside an active Kids session, **`ageBand` comes from the Kids session claims and overrides any query-string value**.
- `GET /api/kids/playlists` & `GET /api/kids/playlists/:id` — Returns curated allowlisted playlists (**re-checking live visibility and current `staff_channel_ids_json` membership so removing a channel from `staff_channel_ids_json` immediately excludes its playlists**; **`GET /api/kids/playlists`** omits any playlist with zero approved visible videos; **`GET /api/kids/playlists/:id`** returns the playlist along with **only its videos that are individually approved in `kids_catalog_items` (`status = 'approved'`, matching active `ageBand`) and visible in `search_videos`**—unapproved or out-of-band videos are silently omitted, never errored or auto-admitted). Under an active Kids session, **`ageBand` comes from the session claims**. **Kids playlist shape (`S-K`):** returns `{ id, title, titleLocalized, description, descriptionLocalized, channelId, channelName, channelNameLocalized, approvedVideosCount, approvedCoverUrl }` with URLs/links stripped from descriptions, `approvedCoverUrl` derived strictly from the first approved visible video in the active age band, `approvedVideosCount` counting only approved visible videos in the active age band, and **never** exposes `coverVideoIds`, raw unapproved `thumbnailUrl`, total `videosCount`, unapproved `videos` IDs, or `userId`.
- `GET /api/me/parental-controls` & `PUT /api/me/parental-controls` — Returns `{ hasPin, pinUpdatedAt, pinLockedUntil, pinHardLocked, activeAgeBand, dailyLimitMinutes, bedtimeStart, bedtimeEnd, bedtimeTimezone, prayerPauseEnabled, updatedAt }` (**never** exposes `pin_hash` or `pin_salt`). Updating settings while a PIN is set requires valid `pin` (checked with the same atomic lockout counter).
- `PUT /api/me/parental-controls/pin` — Set or change 4–6 digit PIN (requires fresh **interactive** Auth SSO step-up via `prompt=login` and `auth_time <= 300s`, plus `currentPin` if supplied; resets lockout and `pin_hard_locked`, and bumps `kids_session_epoch`).
- `POST /api/me/kids-mode/enter` — Enters Kids Mode for the active device/browser session, locking the Auth sign-in session and swapping for a revocable `kids:viewer` session persisted in `kids_sessions` (`KIDS-D13`).
- `POST /api/me/kids-mode/exit` — Atomically claims attempts before PBKDF2 and verifies parental PIN with **escalating lockout** (5th failure → 5 min, 6th → 15 min, 7th → 1 h, **8th failure** → `pin_hard_locked` hard stop; a correct PIN on the 8th attempt still succeeds.). Triggers `UPDATE … SET pin_hard_locked = 1 WHERE user_id = ? AND pin_hard_locked = 0 RETURNING user_id` so concurrent bursts send **at most one** security alert email, delivered via the **shared branded email layout** and **configured sender** in **one language only** (the parent's saved language preference, falling back to the request UI language). Also accepts interactive Auth SSO `auth_time` (`<= 300s`); revokes the Kids session and restores the adult session.
- `POST /api/studio/channels/:channelId/kids-review` — Creator submission to Kids review queue (**`targetType = 'video'` only**, requires channel editor/owner role and visible video belonging to `channelId`).

- **Kids Mode route-classification & WebSocket tests (`E20-4`):** Iterates every non-internal route registered in `api-videos` (`listServedRoutes()`); fails CI if any route lacks an explicit `KIDS_ROUTE_POLICIES` entry (`block_403`, `suppress_attribution`, `allowlisted_only`, `allow_public`, or `admin_internal`), verifies `GET /api/me/features` forces `listenMode: false` and `offlineDownloads: false`, verifies E14 audio/download/sync routes and E17 live/chat/reminder routes return `403 kids_mode_restricted`, verifies `websocket-server` (`apps/websocket-server/src/auth.ts`) refuses `kids:viewer` tokens, and checks `admin-api` `POST /internal/errors` ingestion scrubbing under Kids Mode.
- **Behavioral write-path, sanitization & web proxy tests:** `E20-2` & `E20-4` verify Kids catalog filtering (**channels omitted** until channel admission semantics are built and shipped; `POST /api/kids/catalog`, review approval, and Studio review submit refuse `channel` and non-staff playlists; removing a channel from `staff_channel_ids_json` immediately excludes its playlists from `GET /api/kids/catalog`, `GET /api/kids/playlists`, and `GET /api/kids/playlists/:id`; curated playlists use the sanitized Kids playlist shape and return **only individually approved & visible videos** with unapproved/empty playlists omitted; both videos and playlists use strict payload allow-lists and strip URLs/bare domains from `description` & `descriptionLocalized`; **editing or replacing an approved (or trashed `pre_delete_status = 'approved'`) video's title, description, localized metadata, thumbnail, media, audio tracks, captions/subtitles, or chapters fails closed on D1 error and synchronously revokes `kids_catalog_items` (`status` / `pre_delete_status = 'revoked'`) across every write path**; and **session `ageBand` overrides query-string filters**). `E20-4` further verifies under an active Kids Mode session zero D1/R2/Queue writes from `POST /api/videos/:id/view` (`discovery_viewers` & `viewerId`), `PUT /api/watch-state/:videoId`, `POST /api/watch-history*`, `POST /api/analytics/events`, `POST /internal/errors` (user ID stripped, `/kids/` URL scrubbed), log redaction (no `userId`, template `route`), and `403 kids_mode_restricted` on social writes, `PUT /api/devices/:installId`, `/api/library/*`, `/api/notifications*`, `/api/search`, `/api/channels/:id/*`, `/api/playlists/:id*`. `E20-5` tests `apps/web` server proxy routing of all browser Kids-Mode API calls (`X-Kids-Mode: 1`) & host-only `HttpOnly` cookie session swap.
- **PIN security & GDPR completeness tests (`E20-4`):** PBKDF2 hash non-exposure (`GET /api/me/parental-controls` & GDPR export omit `pin_hash`/`pin_salt`), `PUT /api/me/parental-controls` PIN check when set, `auth_time <= 300s` step-up check, atomic parallel-request failure claiming, escalating lockout (`5m → 15m → 1h → hard stop` on 8th **failure** while a **valid PIN on the 8th try succeeds**), **exactly one** single-language branded alert email sent via configured sender per hard lock under concurrent burst, SSO exit, GDPR export & indexed GDPR erase across `parental_controls`, `kids_sessions`, `kids_review_requests`, and `kids_catalog_audit`.
